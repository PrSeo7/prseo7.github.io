# Tabata Timer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-09_

Tabata Timer (the “App”) is developed by PrSeo7 (“we”, “us”). This policy describes what the App does with data on iOS and Android. It is a description, not a request for consent: where the law requires your consent (for personalized ads), the App asks for it separately, as described in section 5. The App has no accounts, and we run no server of our own. If you do not want the data collection described here, you can buy **Premium** (no advertising data at all) or uninstall the App (no collection at all).

PrSeo7 is the data controller for the processing described below, except where this policy says that Google, Apple or Google Play acts on its own behalf. Contact details are in section 10.

---

## 1. What the App Collects

### 1.1 Data stored on your device
- Your workouts, interval settings, beep, haptics and language preferences, and whether Premium is owned are stored in the App’s preferences on your device. We cannot access them.
- Your operating system may include this data in its backups (Android backup to your Google account, iOS backups to iCloud or a computer). That is governed by Google’s or Apple’s terms, and it means a reinstall on the same account can bring the data back.

### 1.2 Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics, by Google)
- Runs from the first launch, for every user, Premium included. It records which features you use (tab changes, workout start/pause/reset/finish, timer runs, preset changes, the beep toggle, Premium purchase and restore actions including the price, currency and store transaction number of a completed purchase) together with your device model, operating system version and language, and the country or region that Google derives from a masked IP address. No location services are used.
- Events are tied to an installation identifier that Google assigns to this copy of the App (and, on iOS, to Apple’s identifier for vendors), not to your name or an account. We cannot map that identifier to a person.
- The App does not give Analytics your Advertising ID or IDFA, and it keeps all advertising-related uses of Analytics switched off. Analytics data is used only for product analytics.

### 1.3 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, by Google)
- When the App crashes, Google receives the stack trace, device model, operating system version, the App’s state at the time, and installation identifiers assigned by Google. No user identifier or custom data is attached.

### 1.4 Advertising (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)
- Free users see banner and interstitial ads after an initial ad-free period that starts when you first open the App. Before that period ends nothing ad-related runs and no advertising data is collected.
- To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect your Advertising ID or IDFA (subject to your choices in section 5), IP address and the approximate location inferred from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
- Premium users never see ads and the App never starts the advertising software for them.

### 1.5 Premium purchase
- Premium is a one-time purchase (removes ads and unlocks landscape). Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details; the store tells the App only whether the product is owned.
- The App re-checks ownership with the store at each launch using the store’s own client software. No receipt is sent to us. If the store says the product is not owned, or cannot be reached for several days, Premium is switched off until the store confirms it again. **Restore purchases** in Settings re-reads ownership after a reinstall or on a new device.

### 1.6 Device permissions
- **Notifications**: to alert you when a workout or timer finishes while the App is in the background. The alert is scheduled on your device; no push service is involved.
- **Tracking (iOS)**: may be requested as part of the ad consent flow, only to decide whether ads can use your IDFA. The App works if you deny it.
- **Audio and keep-awake**: to play beeps and keep the screen on during a workout. No recordings are made.

The App does not collect contacts, precise location, health data or payment information.

## 2. Why We Process Data and the Legal Bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Run the timer, keep your settings | On-device data (1.1) | None needed: we never receive it |
| Understand which features are used and fix crashes | Analytics and crash data (1.2, 1.3) | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): improving a free app. Safeguards: no accounts, no advertising identifier, masked IP, automatic deletion after 14 months / 90 days, uninstalling ends collection |
| Show ads that fund the free version | Advertising data (1.4) | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for ads in general; **your consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalized ads, given or refused in the consent dialog and changeable at any time (section 5) |
| Sell and honour Premium | Purchase status (1.5) | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); Apple and Google process the payment as independent controllers |

We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. Providing this data is not required by law or by a contract with us; the only consequence of refusing is that you cannot use the App, or its free version.

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Retention

- **On your device**: until you clear the App’s storage in your operating system settings or uninstall it. Backups follow Google’s or Apple’s retention. Ownership of Premium is kept by Apple or Google under your store account and can be restored at any time.
- **Analytics**: Google deletes user-level analytics data no later than 14 months after collection.
- **Crash reports**: Google deletes crash data after 90 days.
- **Advertising**: Google anonymizes advertising data in its server logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months and identifier information after 18 months ([source](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention)).

Because analytics and crash data carry no identifier we control, we cannot find a specific person’s records to delete them individually. They are deleted automatically at the end of these periods, and uninstalling the App stops all further collection.

## 5. Your Choices

- **Ad consent**: where your region requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent dialog appears the first time you open the App after the ad-free period, before any ad is requested. Until you answer, no ads are shown. You can review or change your answer at any time under **Privacy options** in the App’s Settings tab. New ad requests follow the changed choice; an ad already on screen may stay until the next launch. Outside those regions the entry is not shown.
- **Tracking (iOS)**: the ad consent flow may show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can deny or revoke it at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. When tracking is not allowed, ads are not based on your IDFA.
- **Platform ad controls**: reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device settings. Ads will still be shown, without using that identifier.
- **Remove ads**: buying **Premium** removes ads and the App stops starting the advertising software. Analytics and crash reporting continue.
- **Analytics**: there is no in-app switch. Uninstalling the App ends collection.
- **Local data**: clear the App’s storage in your operating system settings. Premium can be brought back with **Restore purchases**.

## 6. Your Rights

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to withdraw consent for personalized ads at any time (section 5). Because the analytics and crash data hold no identifier that lets us recognise you, we cannot fulfil access, erasure or portability requests for that data (GDPR Article 11); the effective controls are the ones in section 5. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority: [EEA authorities](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en), the UK [ICO](https://ico.org.uk), the Swiss [FDPIC](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch).

If you are a California resident: we do not sell personal data, and we collect no sensitive personal information. Personalized advertising may count as “sharing”; you can refuse it through the controls in section 5 or by buying Premium. You may contact us at the address in section 10 with any request, and we will not discriminate against you for making one.

## 7. Children
